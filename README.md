## 📌 Project Mission

**ARMONIA** is a decentralized, zero-cost, open-source community framework designed to be downloaded and deployed by any church, parish, NGO, or civic group worldwide. 

Its primary purpose is to prevent extreme isolation, somatic trauma degradation, and silent crises by providing three core pillars:
1. **Unconditional Daily Food** (a loaf of bread / a warm meal) with no paperwork or questions asked.
2. **A Neutral Sanctuary Space** for rest and empathetic listening (de-medicalized, non-bureaucratic).
3. **A 24/7 Helpline & Dispatch System** operated by trained local volunteers.
